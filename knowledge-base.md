## 02 · IDEAL CLIENT AVATAR — "The Unapologetic Builder"

### Demographics
- Age: 28–45
- Income: $100K–$250K+
- Career: Corporate leader, senior professional, founder, consultant, executive-track
- Wealth stage: Mid-level builder — earning well, not yet operating as a wealth architect
- Net worth: $50K–$500K, not optimized
- Education: Highly educated, high performer
- Location: Urban or affluent suburban markets
- Investment knowledge: Surface-level awareness, not strategic confidence

### Who She Is — Core Identity
She is not average. She has never been average.
She knows she is capable of more — but doesn't know the next move.
She has built real things. Success hasn't felt like enough. She can't name exactly why.

**Her deepest drive:** To lead a life that makes her proud, not just successful.

### Her Pain Points (The Real Depth)

**Under-Activation**
She is earning well but not fully expressed.
The quiet frustration: "I should be further by now." Not externally — internally.
She sees women playing smaller than her and making more.

**Intellectual Confidence, Emotional Ceiling**
She understands numbers. She can read a spreadsheet.
But when it comes to demanding more? Negotiating boldly? Investing aggressively?
There is hesitation. Her nervous system is still calibrating to expansion.

**Isolation at the Top**
She has friends — but not a room full of women who:
Talk openly about money. Want millions. Think long-term. Refuse to shrink.
She is craving proximity to women who normalize ambition.

**Fear of Wasted Potential**
This is the real one.
She is afraid of playing safe. Living comfortably but not fully.
Being "successful" but not legendary. Looking back knowing she didn't go all in.
She is not afraid of hard work. She is afraid of under-living.

**The Internal Standard**
She holds herself to high self-imposed standards.
She feels pressure to perform and wants to prove to herself what she's capable of.
She wants to win — but on her terms.

### Her Desires (Heart-Level)
- Seven-figure wealth without chaos
- To negotiate without flinching
- To see six-figure savings and not panic
- To hold wealth calmly
- To build generational assets
- To retire early if she chooses
- To attend retreats where women talk net worth, not gossip or trends
- To say her income out loud and feel neutral
- To stop hoping and start deciding
- **Most importantly: to feel congruent with her power**

### What She Buys — and Doesn't

She does NOT buy:
- Cheap mindset fluff
- Pinterest-level budgeting advice
- Beginner money education

She DOES buy:
- Frameworks · Strategy · Rooms with high-caliber women
- Structured transformation · Cohorts (not self-paced chaos)
- Identity-based positioning

Price psychology:
- At $795 → feels accessible
- At $1,295 → feels aligned with her identity. She will respect the higher price.

## 05 · THE WEALTH ALIGNMENT — PROGRAM DATA

### Overview
8-week financial leadership cohort for ambitious women earning $100K+ who are ready to stop staying comfortable and start leading their wealth with structure, standards, and strategic expansion.

This is not a budgeting course.
This is psychological infrastructure + tactical execution.

### Positioning Statement
The Wealth Alignment is the financial pillar inside Legacy Collective — a cohort program for ambitious women ready to lead their wealth with identity, structure, and expansion.

### The Core Method
Identity → Language → Standards → Capacity → Expansion → Integration

### The Four Income Pillars
- Self-Worth — internal fee ceiling, what she believes she deserves
- Standards — what she accepts from clients, partners, herself
- Skill — the competencies that actually generate income
- Safety — her nervous system's relationship with money and risk

### Core Program Assets
- Money Mirror Assessment — reveals current money identity; protected inside the program, not a public lead magnet
- Identity Gap Exercise — maps distance between current and target wealth identity
- Wealth Snapshot — financial baseline: net worth, income sources, beliefs, spending
- Companion Tracker Tools — four pillar worksheets distributed in GHL community

### Curriculum
**Module 1: Wealth Identity Foundation**
Core question: Who are you in relation to money right now?
Tools: Money Mirror Assessment, Wealth Snapshot, Identity Gap Exercise
Key teaching: The version of you who has wealth doesn't think about money the way you currently do.
Power quote: "Tomorrow is the most dangerous word in your financial vocabulary."
Format: Live lecture, facilitator-guided. Loose bullet script, not teleprompter.

**Module 2: W.A.I.T. — Story**
Addresses the narratives she carries about money and earning.

**Modules 3–8:** Receiving, Language, Standards, Capacity, Expansion, Integration
— Update as each module is built —

### Transformation Outcome
The most noticeable shift after Wealth Alignment: Certainty.
Not hype. Not motivation. Certainty.
She becomes a woman who knows she can build wealth on her terms.

### Cohort 1 — Observed Shifts (After 2 Modules)
- Opens credit card statements instead of avoiding them
- Calculates net worth for the first time, without emotional charge
- Identifies the income pillar that is capping her wealth
- Names and rewrites the belief holding her ceiling in place
- Installs a new identity around wealth
- Begins mapping a path to $1M+ strategically
