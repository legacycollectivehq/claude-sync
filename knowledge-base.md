# Legacy Collective HQ — Knowledge Base Summary

## Brand Essence
Legacy Collective positions itself as a platform where ambitious women expand through ownership and strategic wealth alignment. The core philosophy: "Aligned ambition compounds. Misaligned ambition depletes."

## Ideal Client
The Unapologetic Builder — a 28–45-year-old earning $100K–$250K+ who feels under-activated despite external success. She craves intellectual rigor around wealth, isolation at the top, and fears wasted potential. She rejects beginner-level content and responds to frameworks, strategy, and cohorts with high-caliber women.

## Brand Voice
Calm authority — measured, direct, elegant without being stiff. Copy should feel like it comes from "a woman who has already made the decision." The tone activates rather than soothes, emphasizing certainty over motivation.

**USE:** Financial leadership, wealth standard, identity-led earning, calm capacity, million-dollar integration, expand without apology, congruence, standards, expansion, ownership.

**AVOID:** Hustle, grind, boss babe, girlboss, manifest millions, abundance vibes, soft life, toxic positivity, "you deserve it" without structure, heal your money wounds, break scarcity.

**Writing rules (non-negotiable):**
- No em dashes in any content
- Never use the word "behind" to describe the avatar (she is uncapped, unstructured, or not yet calibrated — never behind)
- Never say "$1.5M" — always say "crossed seven figures" or "seven-figure net worth"
- Sentences should flow with natural rhythm, not short choppy bursts

## Visual Identity
Forest green (#003d20) anchors legacy and authority. Gold (#c0a54a) suggests wealth without flash. Steel Blue (#4e708f) and Citrine (#ebd167) are secondary accents. Linen (#f8eee0) and Ivory (#fffaf5) are background tones.

Typography pairs Lora (serif, display headlines and quotes) with Poppins (body copy, labels, eyebrows). The aesthetic evokes refined institutional presence — Martha's Vineyard polish, not Miami influencer energy.

## Core Program: The Wealth Alignment
An 8-week cohort for ambitious women earning $100K+ who are ready to stop staying comfortable and start leading their wealth with structure, standards, and strategic expansion. Method: Identity → Language → Standards → Capacity → Expansion → Integration.

This is not budgeting. It is calibration — psychological infrastructure plus tactical systems.
